Emploid
==========

Windows:  double-click  emploid.exe
Linux:    open a terminal in this folder and run  ./emploid

A window opens (leave it open while you use the app) and your browser shows the app at http://127.0.0.1:8765.
Close the window, or press Ctrl+C in it, to stop the app.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed.
Choose "More info" and then "Run anyway". Your antivirus may also ask: the app is plain Python packed into a folder.

Your resume, answers and results stay on this computer (see the data folder with:  emploid data-dir).
Searching works immediately. Auto-apply needs a one-time browser download (about 150 MB), which the app offers when you use it.

Help and source code: see the project page.
